I wrote a blog post for [SeatGeek's Dev
blog](http://seatgeek.com/blog/dev/) about my recent work
[automating image cropping with
OpenCV](http://seatgeek.com/blog/dev/opencv-face-detection-for-cropping-faces).
We use this to generate images for our iPhone app, upcoming
iPad app, and throughout our site.

It reached [top 10 on Hacker
News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=5906302) and was
generating 20% of the traffic to our site for a period of time
after it was submitted. Given it's success I'm working on a
post regarding recent changes I've made to our deploy process
as well.
