Friday August 17th was my last day at [AWeber](http://aweber.com). I've
accepted a position at [SeatGeek](http://seatgeek.com) as a Web Engineer.

AWeber was a great opportunity that turned me from a programmer into a
developer. I wish everyone there the best of luck.

Now that I've left I'm upset that most of what I worked on there was not
open source so I will no longer be able to use the things that I've built.
Towards the end of my time there I was trying to put more projects on Github
that could have been useful to someone. Hopefully at SeatGeek I will have
the change to make some larger contributions!
