I've been using [Google Music](http://music.google.com/) quite a bit lately. It
was rather painless to setup although the upload process can take a while. The
service works out well with my Macbook Air since I only have a 64GB SSD and
don't want to fill my drive with music I may or may not be listening to very
frequently.

The only issue for me is that the Flash performance can frequently cause
studdering if I am doing too much in Chrome at the same time.
